# Privacy Policy for PhotoReport

**Last Updated: July 31, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how PhotoReport ("we", "us", or "our") handles your personal information.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
PhotoReport is designed as a **privacy-first, offline-capable tool**. 

*   **Media Access**: The app requires access to your device's camera to capture photos and videos for your reports. All captured media is stored locally in the app's private storage or your device's DCIM folder.
*   **Location Data**: If you enable the "GPS Watermark" feature, the app will request access to your device's location. This data is used solely to overlay coordinates and address information onto your photos. This processing happens entirely on your device.
*   **No Server Uploads**: We do not maintain any servers. Your projects, notes, photos, and location data are never transmitted to us or any third party.

## 2. Storage and Backups
All data is stored on your device. When you use the "Backup" feature, a ZIP file is created on your device which you can manually move or share. We do not have access to these backups.

## 3. Data Safety
We do not collect, share, or sell any user data. The app does not use any third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

## 4. Permissions
*   **CAMERA**: Required to take photos for reports.
*   **ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION**: Required for the optional GPS Watermark feature.
*   **POST_NOTIFICATIONS**: Used to show the progress and completion of project exports.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
